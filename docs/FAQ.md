# FASOSH — Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. What is FASOSH?

FASOSH is a healthcare operating platform being developed by FASRED Technologies Ltd. Its vision is to connect patient journeys, clinical services, healthcare resources, and operational workflows within one configurable system.

## 2. What does “The Operating System for Healthcare” mean?

It describes the FASOSH vision: a connected platform through which healthcare organisations can coordinate relevant activities across patient care, clinical departments, facilities, resources, and administration.

It does not mean that every healthcare function is already available in the current product.

## 3. Who is FASOSH designed for?

FASOSH is intended to serve healthcare organisations of different sizes and types, including:

- Primary healthcare centres and general practices
- Specialist clinics
- Pharmacies and diagnostic facilities
- Maternity and rehabilitation services
- General and teaching hospitals
- Multi-facility healthcare organisations
- Healthcare networks and public healthcare programmes

The capabilities required will depend on each organisation's services and operational needs.

## 4. Is FASOSH only for large hospitals?

No. The product vision includes small healthcare providers as well as larger hospitals and multi-facility organisations.

The intention is to allow each organisation to use the relevant capabilities without requiring every customer to adopt the same structure or complexity.

## 5. What healthcare workflows is FASOSH intended to support?

The intended scope spans relevant stages of the healthcare lifecycle, including:

- Patient arrival and registration
- Appointments, assessment, and consultation
- Clinical documentation and care coordination
- Diagnostic investigations and results
- Treatment and medication workflows
- Billing, payments, and healthcare coverage
- Admission, inpatient care, and transfers
- Referral, discharge, and follow-up
- Operational reporting and organisational oversight

Availability depends on the actual implementation and the scope of the relevant release.

## 6. Can FASOSH support emergency departments?

Emergency coordination and patient-flow visibility are important areas of the product vision.

Intended capabilities include relevant queue visibility, workload coordination, resource awareness, and handover workflows. Prospective customers should confirm which emergency-related capabilities are currently available before using the system in a live clinical environment.

FASOSH does not replace qualified professionals' clinical judgement or responsibility for patient care.

## 7. Can an organisation use only the capabilities it needs?

That is a guiding principle of the product vision.

Different healthcare settings have different workflows and requirements. FASOSH is intended to support configurable organisational structures and relevant capabilities rather than impose one universal workflow on every customer.

The available configuration options should be confirmed for the current release.

## 8. Can FASOSH support multiple facilities?

Multi-facility coordination is part of the intended product direction.

The vision includes appropriate local facility operations and authorised organisation-wide oversight, with permissions and data separation suited to the organisation's structure.

The supported scope should be confirmed before deployment.

## 9. Does FASOSH integrate with laboratories, imaging systems, insurance providers, or payment services?

Interoperability is part of the product direction. Potential integration areas include diagnostic systems, imaging, health insurance and HMO services, payment providers, identity services, and other operational systems.

A particular integration should not be assumed to be available until it has been confirmed.

## 10. Is FASOSH currently available for production use?

FASOSH is under active development. Its complete product vision extends beyond any individual release.

Contact FASRED Technologies to discuss the current implementation, available capabilities, deployment suitability, and any validation required for your intended use.

## 11. How does FASOSH approach patient privacy and security?

Privacy, appropriate access control, organisational data separation, traceability, reliability, and accountability are guiding product principles.

Specific security controls, compliance status, certifications, and deployment arrangements must be confirmed against the actual implementation and applicable requirements. No certification or regulatory approval should be inferred from these principles alone.

## 12. Can FASOSH replace every existing healthcare system?

The long-term vision is to connect relevant healthcare workflows and systems. The appropriate role of FASOSH will depend on an organisation's existing technology, operational requirements, supported integrations, and the capabilities available in the relevant release.

An assessment is recommended before planning a replacement or migration.
