# FASOSH Product Roadmap

FASOSH is being developed toward a connected healthcare operating platform that helps healthcare organisations coordinate patient care, clinical services, resources, and operational activities.

Our product direction is guided by a simple principle: healthcare organisations should be able to coordinate their work through a connected system without being forced into a one-size-fits-all workflow.

This roadmap describes the intended direction of the product. Priorities and availability may change as customer needs, implementation progress, and validation outcomes evolve.

## 1. A Reliable Foundation for Healthcare Operations

**Goal:** Establish the foundation for coordinating healthcare services and patient information.

Product direction includes:

- Configurable healthcare organisations and facilities
- User access and role-appropriate permissions
- Core patient and encounter workflows
- Relevant clinical and administrative records
- Reliable handling of essential healthcare activities

The aim is to support the workflows required by each organisation's initial deployment and provide a foundation for further capabilities.

## 2. Connected Patient Journeys

**Goal:** Connect the different stages of a patient's journey rather than treating each service as an isolated system.

Product direction includes:

- Patient arrival, registration, and appointments
- Assessment, consultation, and care documentation
- Requests for diagnostic and treatment services
- Coordination of relevant patient and service information
- Referral, discharge, and follow-up workflows

The intended outcome is better continuity of information and coordination across the services involved in patient care.

## 3. Patient Flow and Emergency Coordination

**Goal:** Improve operational visibility and coordination, particularly in busy and time-critical healthcare settings.

Product direction includes:

- Patient queues and service status
- Visibility into relevant workloads and resource availability
- Coordination across emergency and other clinical services
- Handover and outstanding-activity visibility
- Appropriate operational notifications and escalation workflows

These capabilities are intended to support healthcare professionals and operational teams. They do not replace clinical judgement or guarantee that an alert has been acted upon.

## 4. Connected Clinical and Support Services

**Goal:** Connect the services that support patient care within each organisation.

Depending on the healthcare setting, product direction includes workflows for:

- Laboratory and diagnostic services
- Imaging and related services
- Pharmacy and medication processes
- Inpatient care, wards, and beds
- Procedures and other specialist services
- Billing, payments, and healthcare coverage
- Referrals and discharge coordination

The applicable services will depend on organisational needs and the capabilities included in each release.

## 5. Flexible Healthcare Organisations

**Goal:** Support healthcare providers of different sizes without imposing unnecessary complexity.

Product direction includes support for:

- Specialist practices and small clinics
- Primary healthcare centres
- Pharmacies and diagnostic facilities
- General and teaching hospitals
- Multi-facility healthcare organisations
- Healthcare networks and public healthcare programmes

Organisations should be able to configure relevant departments, units, services, permissions, and workflows to reflect how they operate.

## 6. Multi-Facility Coordination and Oversight

**Goal:** Support organisations that operate across multiple facilities or organisational levels.

Product direction includes:

- Appropriate local facility operations
- Authorised organisation-wide oversight
- Relevant cross-facility reporting
- Configurable organisational structures
- Appropriate separation of data and permissions

The objective is to enable coordination where it is authorised and useful while maintaining the independence and privacy requirements of different organisations.

## 7. Interoperability and Connected Systems

**Goal:** Enable FASOSH to work with relevant external systems rather than requiring every organisation to replace all its existing technology.

Potential integration areas include:

- Laboratory, imaging, and diagnostic systems
- Health insurance and HMO services
- Payment providers
- Identity and communication services
- Accounting and operational systems
- Relevant healthcare data-exchange standards

Each integration depends on technical feasibility, partner support, security review, applicable requirements, and validation. Inclusion in this roadmap does not mean an integration is currently available.

## 8. Reporting and Continuous Improvement

**Goal:** Help authorised users understand healthcare operations and identify opportunities to improve services.

Product direction includes:

- Relevant operational and service reports
- Appropriate organisational oversight
- Visibility into workflow progress and outstanding activities
- Useful measures of service performance
- Responsible development of analytics and decision-support capabilities

Reporting should be based on reliable data, clear definitions, appropriate access, and respect for patient privacy.

## How We Prioritise

Product priorities are guided by:

1. Patient safety and clinical accountability.
2. The practical needs of healthcare professionals and patients.
3. Reliability and usability in real operating environments.
4. Customer requirements and validated workflows.
5. Privacy, security, and applicable regulatory obligations.
6. Evidence of meaningful improvements in healthcare operations.
7. Sustainable development and support across different organisational sizes.

## Product Availability

FASOSH is under active development. This roadmap communicates the intended product direction rather than a guaranteed delivery schedule.

Individual capabilities may be at different stages of development, validation, release, or availability. Prospective customers should confirm the current scope before relying on a capability for operational use.

We aim to develop FASOSH incrementally, prioritising practical value while working toward the broader vision of a connected healthcare operating platform.
